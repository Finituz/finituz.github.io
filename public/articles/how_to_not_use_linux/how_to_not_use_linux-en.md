# How Not to Use Linux

## An Ode to Learning Something New While Clinging to the Old!

Didn’t understand the title and subtitle? Well, to understand, we need to go back a bit. To when we were children...  
Do you remember the last time you learned something? How was it? What did it feel like? When we’re young, everything is new, and that has a huge advantage!  
Since we had no prior reference, what was presented to us initially evoked curiosity, and from that feeling, once we got used to it, we understood that it was the only way to do that thing.

> Learning from absolute zero is a delightful **blessing**, but also an even greater **curse**.

Look, reflect on our beloved free software, **Linux**. Are you, migrating from **_Windows_** or **_Mac OS_**, being fair to the penguin?  
And let’s remember that Linux is just a **_kernel_**, the core of the system. Okay, I know you understand Linux as this entire **ecosystem**...  
However, thinking this way, the dynamics are different. The **open-source** world, despite my criticisms, is primarily based on the willingness of the people involved to keep these projects alive. Meanwhile, the others are complete systems managed by billion-dollar companies.

Of course, **_let’s not be naive_**, the **Linux kernel** receives millions in donations. In fact, **Linus Torvalds**, the creator of Linux, is a billionaire. However, not everyone who participates in maintaining the penguin’s core receives a share of these donations. Not only that, but maintainers of essential software for huge operating systems like Windows don’t even receive support from these large corporations. Let’s discuss this "free software for all" and its contradictions in future articles.

Evaluate the ~situation~, why does something with the same purpose as what you’re used to work differently? I remember when I started using VIM, it was very different from all the other editors I had used, and I kept wondering why I should use something like that. Nowadays, I confess I can’t use an editor that doesn’t at least have **_VIM Mode_** haha.

> Try something, no matter how different it is! Author: any **"coach"** from a bar.

Whenever you open a folder with a single click in **KDE 5.0** or because **Gnome** only comes with a close button on windows... Think, maybe it’s time to learn a new way of doing something.
